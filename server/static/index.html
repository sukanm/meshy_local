Advanced options

Both sliders only affect output when the repair stage has to fall back to voxel remeshing to make a mesh watertight (shown in the report below as "voxel_remesh_used") — most jobs don't hit this path at all.

Generates this many reconstructions with different seeds and keeps the cleanest one (by topology-defect count) — a real quality win found by direct testing (same source image, one seed had 87% of its surface below the wall-thickness floor, another had 22%), at the cost of roughly multiplying image-to-3D generation time. 1 disables this and generates once, the old behavior.

Print prep

Thickening grows the whole surface outward by the offset, so every wall is at least 2× the offset thick. More offset = sturdier but blunter spikes: 0.3mm keeps points sharp and prints with Arachne walls; 0.6mm guarantees the full 1.2mm wall but rounds fine tips. Every job is also sliced in Bambu Studio (P2S, PLA, 0.12mm layers, Arachne walls, tree supports) — open the .3mf to print with those settings.
